Questions

Will it quote the wrong price and cost me money?

No. It estimates only from the price ranges you enter, always as a range (never a single number), and every estimate ends with "this is a rough ballpark, not a final quote." If a job isn't in your list, it says so and captures the lead anyway.

How do quote requests reach me?

Instantly by email — the moment a visitor asks for an exact quote, you get their name, number, job description, the ballpark they saw, their preferred visit date/time if they picked one, and any photo they uploaded (attached). Everything is also saved to your owner dashboard.

Do I have to use the photo and booking features?

No. They're on by default but visitors can skip the photo and the visit date — the core estimate-and-lead flow works the same either way. Service area check only kicks in if you enter ZIP codes.

Does it work with my website builder?

Yes — WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, or plain HTML. If you can paste a line of code (or have someone who can), it works.

What happens when I cancel?

The widget is removed and billing stops. No lock-in, no fees.

Can I change my prices later?

Anytime. Update your ranges and the estimator uses them immediately.
